Ordering the parts

  1. On BrickLink, open Wanted → Upload and paste bricklink/wanted_list_complete.xml. Use the per-book files to buy one model at a time.
  2. Open the new list and press Buy All. Let BrickLink pick the fewest shops, condition Any.
  3. Check the items marked in parts_list.csv (dark blue roof parts, plain horse barding, cape). Each note names a substitute colour or part.
  4. Build from the PDFs in instructions/, in book order. models/ opens in BrickLink Studio, LeoCAD or LDCad.
